About

Built for the shop, not the showroom.

Bargain Vape exists because the gap between a cheap disposable and a good one got too wide. We closed it — a genuinely premium 2-gram pen, packaged so it is ready to sell the moment it reaches the counter.

The Idea

The name is the promise.

Most of what lands on a smoke shop counter is one of two things: a well-made pen priced out of the impulse range, or a cheap one that gets returned. Neither is good for the shop.

We built Bargain Vape around the middle of that gap — two full grams of hemp-derived distillate, a device that actually feels good in the hand, and packaging that earns its spot on the counter, with the price printed right on the box.

The neon is deliberate. A product that reads from across the store gets picked up, and a matte black box lit with neon does that better than anything else on the shelf.

What We Hold To

Four things we don't cut

The oil

Hemp-derived distillate with strain-matched terpenes, tested by an accredited third-party lab every batch. Certificates of analysis go to any stocking retailer who asks.

The hardware

A matte-black body, a real cartridge window and a draw that pulls the same on the last gram as the first. Cheap hardware is the fastest way to lose a repeat customer.

The packaging price

$19.99 is printed on the box, and it is the same on every flavor and every case. One number, printed everywhere, so nothing has to be explained at the counter.

The packaging

Every case is designed to open into its own display. You should never have to build a fixture, print a sign, or find shelf space for something that came in a plain brown box.
